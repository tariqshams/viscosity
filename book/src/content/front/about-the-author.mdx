Normally an author would write this in the third person, as the publisher may not know much about the author and can't write it for them, but I will write it in the first person.

I'm Tariq Shams, a person who has always been fascinated by Science, Technology, Engineering and Math (yes, the entire acronym).

People aren't their job titles or degrees, but I am a Senior Software and AI Engineer who has worked professionally in finance and gaming. I have done iOS development, pure backend, pure frontend and full stack; worked on developer tools; built ML models from scratch (including LLMs); built agentic workflows; and fine-tuned LLMs in production, with evals. I came first in the NVIDIA GTC 2026 Vibe Hackathon, receiving a signed RTX 5080 from NVIDIA's CEO, Jensen Huang, and presented the architecture on an NVIDIA livestream.

I have a BS in Computer Science with a minor in Math, and an MBA, which I earned while working at a bank as an engineer, because I really wanted to understand the business in more depth.

Back in my Computer Science undergrad, before the first version of ChatGPT was released, I won first place in my college's engineering competition by creating an AI music generator that modeled music on a sheet as a language task with a temporal element. I also built a compiler for a subset of Java, having taken theoretical computer science (automata theory) under the late and sadly missed Prof. [Lawrence L. Larmore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_L._Larmore), and Compilers under Prof. Jan "Matt" Bækgaard Pedersen, who wrote *Practical Compiler Construction* (the phoenix book, not the dragon book).

All that to say, while I am not making compilers in my sleep (I have a lot of respect for you if you are such a person), I hadn't found anything like what I had built, hence this book exists. Hopefully it is enjoyable.
